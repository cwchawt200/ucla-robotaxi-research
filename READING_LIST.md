Reading List
Papers grouped by theme. Entries were checked against arXiv, publisher or proceedings pages; venues marked (*) were recalled from standard references and are worth a quick double-check before you cite them formally.
1. Cooperative perception: core methods
Xu, R., Xiang, H., Tu, Z., Xia, X., Yang, M.-H., Ma, J. V2X-ViT: Vehicle-to-Everything Cooperative Perception with Vision Transformer. ECCV 2022. arXiv:2203.10638. (Directly from Prof. Ma's group; a good first baseline.)
Xu, R., Tu, Z., Xiang, H., Shao, W., Zhou, B., Ma, J. CoBEVT: Cooperative Bird's Eye View Semantic Segmentation with Sparse Transformers. CoRL 2022. (Co-authored by Prof. Ma and Prof. Bolei Zhou.)
Hu, Y., Fang, S., Lei, Z., et al. Where2comm: Communication-Efficient Collaborative Perception via Spatial Confidence Maps. NeurIPS 2022. arXiv:2209.12836.
Li, Y., et al. Learning Distilled Collaboration Graph for Multi-Agent Perception (DiscoNet). NeurIPS 2021.
Wang, T.-H., et al. V2VNet: Vehicle-to-Vehicle Communication for Joint Perception and Prediction. ECCV 2020 (*).
Xiang, H., Xu, R., Ma, J. HM-ViT: Hetero-modal Vehicle-to-Vehicle Cooperative Perception with Vision Transformer. arXiv:2304.10628 (2023).
2. Datasets and benchmarks
Xu, R., Xiang, H., Xia, X., Han, X., Li, J., Ma, J. OPV2V: An Open Benchmark Dataset and Fusion Pipeline for Perception with Vehicle-to-Vehicle Communication. ICRA 2022 (*).
Xu, R., et al. V2V4Real: A Real-World Large-Scale Dataset for Vehicle-to-Vehicle Cooperative Perception. CVPR 2023 (*).
Yu, H., Luo, Y., Shu, M., et al. DAIR-V2X: A Large-Scale Dataset for Vehicle-Infrastructure Cooperative 3D Object Detection. CVPR 2022.
Xiang, H., Zheng, Z., Xia, X., et al. V2X-Real: a Large-Scale Dataset for Vehicle-to-Everything Cooperative Perception. arXiv:2403.16034 (2024). (UCLA Mobility Lab; includes denser urban traffic with vulnerable road users.)
Hao, R., et al. RCooper: A Real-World Large-Scale Dataset for Roadside Cooperative Perception. arXiv:2403.10145 (2024).
Sun, P., et al. Scalability in Perception for Autonomous Driving: Waymo Open Dataset. CVPR 2020 (*).
Caesar, H., et al. nuScenes: A Multimodal Dataset for Autonomous Driving. CVPR 2020 (*).
3. Surveys
Towards Vehicle-to-everything Autonomous Driving: A Survey on Collaborative Perception. arXiv:2308.16714 (2023). (Add the author list from the arXiv page when you cite it.)
Vehicle-to-Everything Cooperative Perception for Autonomous Driving. arXiv:2310.03525. (Survey with benchmark comparison tables across DAIR-V2X, V2V4Real, OPV2V.)
4. Simulation and digital twins
Dosovitskiy, A., et al. CARLA: An Open Urban Driving Simulator. CoRL 2017 (*).
Xu, R., et al. OpenCDA: An Open Cooperative Driving Automation Framework Integrated with Co-Simulation. ITSC 2021 (*). (From Prof. Ma's group; see also Runsheng Xu's 2023 UCLA dissertation "Cooperative Driving Automation: Simulation and Perception", on eScholarship.)
Li, Q., Peng, Z., Feng, L., Zhang, Q., Xue, Z., Zhou, B. MetaDrive: Composing Diverse Driving Scenarios for Generalizable Reinforcement Learning. IEEE TPAMI, 2022. arXiv:2109.12674. (Prof. Zhou's group.)
Lopez, P. A., et al. Microscopic Traffic Simulation using SUMO. ITSC 2018 (*).
5. Beyond perception: prediction, planning, safety
Cooperative motion prediction: CMP: Cooperative Motion Prediction with Multi-Agent Communication. arXiv:2403.17916 (2024).
Peng, Z., Li, Q., Liu, C., Zhou, B. Safe Driving via Expert Guided Policy Optimization. CoRL 2021. (Safe learning for driving.)
Kalra, N., Paddock, S. Driving to Safety: How Many Miles of Driving Would It Take to Demonstrate Autonomous Vehicle Reliability? RAND, 2016 (*). (Why validation is hard, so why simulation and twins matter.)
Suggested reading order
Skim one survey (14 or 15) for the landscape.
Read V2X-ViT (1), OPV2V (7) and V2X-Real (10) closely.
Run the open-source baselines for V2X-ViT or Where2comm.
Read the simulation papers (16-18) before building mixed-traffic scenarios.
